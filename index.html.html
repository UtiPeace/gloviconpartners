Common Questions

Honest Answers to Real Doubts

We would rather lose a partner to transparency than gain one through false promises. These are the real questions — answered plainly.

Every business carries risk — and we will not pretend otherwise to win your capital. The primary risks in ecommerce arbitrage include product stalling after importation, logistics failures, and market saturation. We mitigate each one actively:

  • Product stalling: We never run a single product. We maintain a portfolio of winners at all times. A hot product typically takes 6 months to 3 years to stall — long enough to complete multiple profitable cycles before a pivot is needed.
  • Logistics failure: We operate with multiple delivery partners across all 36 states. No single provider failure can ground operations.
  • Market saturation: Our product research is ongoing. We identify replacements before current products peak, not after they decline.

We share this openly because informed partners are better partners. Risk exists — but it is managed, not ignored.

The 10% monthly return is our contractual commitment, backed by a signed partnership agreement — not a projection or a target. Our operating margins of 40–50% mean the business generates well above what is needed to honour this obligation even in a slower month.

That said, we are a trading business — not a bank. Our obligation is legal and binding, honoured from operating revenue. This is why we only accept capital we can responsibly deploy.

Every partner gets a dedicated tracking dashboard showing the active product cycle, project status (sourcing → importation → distribution → live marketing), payment history, next payment date, and order volumes in real time.

Beyond the dashboard, you receive a signed agreement with Glovicon Limited (RC 8731400) and are welcome to request periodic calls with our operations team. We operate with open books — because your confidence is the foundation of a long relationship.

For Growth Partners, your 10% monthly return does not fluctuate with operational performance — it is a fixed contractual obligation regardless of how any given month performs. You bear less operational risk, and in return your upside is capped at the agreed rate.

For Enterprise Partners (₦20M+), your 60% profit share is directly tied to performance. In a strong month your returns can far exceed any fixed rate — but in a weaker month they will reflect that. This tier suits partners who want maximum upside and are comfortable with performance-linked returns.

The 6-month cycle is a structured operational commitment — your capital is deployed into inventory, logistics, and marketing from day one. Early exits are not a built-in feature because unwinding a live cycle mid-operation would harm both parties.
